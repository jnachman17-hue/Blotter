This asset supplements the written specification. It is not final and must not be replicated pixel for pixel.

- Cue-to-Blotter-to-sheet causality is not sufficiently clear.
- Cue-to-row or cue-to-field mapping is not sufficiently clear.
- The vertical Blotter engine is not a settled product diagram.
- The final ownership treatment is not settled.
- Responsive composition must be designed in page context.
- The right side must communicate more clearly how the three Gmail and Calendar activity cues feed through Blotter and update the spreadsheet. The cue cards themselves are directionally acceptable; the causal process is not.
