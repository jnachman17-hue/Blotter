The PNG and HTML remain the visual reference for the spreadsheet, cue cards, Google Sheets density, exact data, and overall content structure. They are not the final implementation and must not be copied where the ratified build specification requires a change.

## Preserve closely

- Google Sheets-native visual language and compact density.
- Current `IB Recruiting Tracker` data and column order.
- Student-maintained fields: Name, Title, Firm.
- Blotter-maintained fields: Status, Next move, Last contact, Days, Call.
- Three activity cues and their exact copy.
- Cue-card visual treatment and relative size.
- Responsibility language: `YOU add the contacts` and `BLOTTER keeps them current`.
- Exact phrase `keeps them current`.
- Spreadsheet dominance and current overall content structure.

## Ratified changes from the visual reference

- Show one current spreadsheet only.
- Remove the stale rear sheet completely from the hero.
- Remove the explicit Blotter engine or intermediary processor.
- Connect each cue directly to the corresponding relationship row's complete maintained block.
- Apply a faint shared Blotter-yellow-family tint across the full Status-through-Call zone for all visible rows.
- Apply stronger maintained-block emphasis to the Sarah Chen, Marcus Lee, and Alex Morgan rows as the three illustrative cue-linked examples.
- Keep Priya Shah and Daniel Kim visibly inside the maintained zone without adding cues.
- Keep both ownership labels below the spreadsheet.
- Use restrained region underlines with small terminals to map the labels to Name-through-Firm and Status-through-Call.
- Optimize and approve the initial composition for desktop web.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not reintroduce:

- tracker-decay or before-and-after storytelling in the hero;
- any stale-sheet fragment;
- a vertical engine rail or central Blotter box;
- ownership labels above the sheet;
- curly braces or large ownership banners;
- isolated-cell-only cue highlighting;
- additional cue cards or metadata;
- comprehension that depends on animation.

The complete layout rules, data package, connector behavior, tint ranges, ownership treatment, Lovable instructions, and desktop acceptance criteria live in `../../ws5-build-specs/01-HERO.md`.
