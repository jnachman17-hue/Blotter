## 1 · The pivot in one page

**The old process, stated honestly:** Idea → discovery calls with recent offer-holders (internal problem validation) → full technical specification and feasibility work → a ratified feature inventory as binding product authority → a complete design system and pixel-level screens of every surface → a landing page built to that spec. Sixteen planning sessions, a deployed app skeleton, a designed system – and zero strangers contacted, zero dollars of market signal, zero observed behavior. Completely backwards.

**The diagnosis:** The process optimized the quality of internal decisions instead of speed to market contact. The specific intellectual error that licensed it: "for a convenience product against a free incumbent, the design layer is the thesis." Design being the thesis is about *winning* a market – it says nothing about whether the market will have you at all, and that second question is cheap to answer and must be answered first. A secondary error: treating discovery interviews as validation. Conversations – especially with peers, especially about willingness to pay – are skewed and structurally incapable of measuring behavior. Assets in front of real strangers, with real intent events captured, are the only instrument.

**The new law:** Test, test, test, and test before you build. Any idea is great in your head; your head is not evidence; the market is. You do not spend a minute or a dollar building something you do not know will work. You put artifacts into the market, measure real behavior with real analytics, learn, and iterate – build-measure-learn, run as fast as the market allows. **The market determines the build.** If there is real signal, invest in an MVP and scale from there. If the market says no, there is no launch. That outcome is a success of the process, not a failure of the project – the entire point is to know before building.

## 2 · Corey's method – the source material

### 2.1 The HONK validation machine

- **He found structurally guaranteed intent.** He bought high-intent click ads: a person in a rural area, on cellular data, searching for a tow truck, is stranded on a road *right now*. Intent was not inferred from demographics – it was guaranteed by context.  
- **He sent that traffic to a landing page for a product that did not exist.** The page carried roughly three different on-demand tow offerings at different price points. He watched what people clicked, captured intent all the way through a buy flow, then told them the service was down and they had not been charged. No product – a landing page intercepting an incumbent's demand, purely to collect data.  
- **Ads ran as tight daily experiments.** Turn ads on in the morning. Stop after a few hours. Read the data. Change the landing page, the offering, the price. Repeat. Iterate constantly, especially early.  
- **He built only what the data called for**, in real time, from real user intent. Then he went physical: pitched actual tow companies, onboarded them, installed his own tablets in their trucks for dispatch.  
- **The terminal artifact of validation is an economics story, not a product.** Prove a dollar produces ninety-three cents out – take that to investors as demonstrated traction and raise capital to close the gap and create efficiency. Product-market fit, demonstrated numerically.

### 2.2 His operating rules

1. **Your opinion is useless. Get market opinion.**  
2. **Start with acquisition:** who is the target user, how do you reach them, what does it cost. This comes before everything.  
3. **Benchmarks before anything runs.** Look up benchmark conversion and click-through rates first, so results have a foundation to compare against and the "learn" phase of each cycle has a defined endpoint – you know when learning is over and it is time to act.  
4. **The decision quantum is roughly 100–200 clicks per landing page variant** before changing the page and testing again. Figure out your actual market and target it efficiently.  
5. **Perfect the message before the page.** Iterate creative until click-through beats benchmark – that proves the message attracts the audience – then send traffic and iterate the landing page.  
6. **A landing page must exist even during message testing.** No ad or post can point at nothing. And the page is a persuasion cascade: the header must be compelling enough to earn the subhead, the subhead earns the scroll, the scroll earns the visual. Attention spans are zero; make them look and read.  
7. **Features before pricing.** Never open with price. Find out what product and features people actually want first; test pricing after.  
8. **Analytics before spend.** Capture number of visitors, the source they came from, every button and action clicked. Put the analytics in place and test them robustly before launching anything, or the money is wasted.  
9. **Small markets iterate slowly.** If you cannot find an inexpensive way to convert a large audience online and get feedback, expand the audience.  
10. **Deck vocabulary that governs the culture:** behavioral analytics over customer development · innovation accounting over vanity metrics · iterate boldly, especially at first · success is binary · pivot or persevere, with three options when returns diminish: iterate boldly again, do customer development, or go another way · big ideas are about thinking small – concierge and smokescreen tests; build and automate later · burn and learn – test assets are sacrificial lambs · QA carefully and fully, lest you lose a cycle · be a scientist.

## 3 · Applying the method to Blotter

### 3.1 What the machine must answer

Prior discovery established that the problem is real and universal but mild: every recruit runs the same tracking loop in a free, adequate, hand-me-down spreadsheet that arrives socially before the problem is felt. That verdict stands as background knowledge. Its operational meaning under the new law: the live question – **will real people show purchase-grade intent for a logistics product over their working spreadsheet** – is behavioral, and it gets answered by real traffic, landing pages, and captured intent events. Nothing else counts.

**The test subject:** undergraduate students recruiting for high finance in the Summer Analyst 2028 class. The student is both user and buyer.

### 3.2 Prior findings demoted to intelligence – inputs to the machine, not rulings

- **No search demand on category terms.** Nobody googles for a recruiting tracker; the category is non-aware and the incumbent spreads by word of mouth. This finding is now load-bearing rather than incidental – see the structural confound in section 7.1.  
- **Seasonality is the market's clock, not ours.** Networking ramps August–October; trackers get seriously compiled in late September; January is the mid-cycle drowning moment. The SA '28 cohort enters the funnel over the coming weeks. These are testing windows and interpretation context – and they set the deadline, not our appetite for preparation.  
- **Go broad first: high finance, not IB.** Testing under "the logistics layer for high finance recruiting" (IB, PE, consulting – functionally the same workflow) enlarges the audience for statistical significance and faster cycles. The IB-versus-broad wedge question becomes testable later through segmented arms rather than being decided by argument.  
- **Positioning intelligence:** the space is saturated with prep courses and AI mass-outreach tools; the logistics-layer identity is the differentiation backdrop for messaging.  
- **Real volume facts from Jon's own cycle** – roughly 500 emails, 80 coffee chats, 35 interview rounds – are true and usable as problem dramatization.  
- **Formerly frozen questions become cheap tests:** web platform versus a Google-Sheets-native companion is now two angles and two thin page variants. Pricing shape becomes on-page price testing – after features. The Gmail-trust question becomes measurable on-page behavior.

### 3.3 Rulings from the pivot conversation

1. **Every prior document and ruling is demoted.** The feature inventory becomes a hypothesis backlog the market votes on. The design system and screens become raw material for creative and page variants. The roadmap, strategy documents, and specs become historical record. Nothing carries authority.  
2. **The existing landing page build is abandoned entirely.** Ground-up rebuild, unconstrained by any technical specification – the page no longer has to be true to a buildable product; it has to be true to the test. Features that cannot yet feasibly be built may be advertised and tested.  
3. **Research precedes all spend.**  
4. **Name runs neutral for testing.** The IB suffix comes off for the broad-audience phase; final naming is deferred until the market speaks.  
5. **Budget: $1,000, amended.** See section 7.2. The figure was ruled insufficient to fund a sequential paid iteration loop. The ceiling is negotiable and paid is demoted to a supporting role.  
6. **Content posture:** smokescreen marketing for a product that does not exist, run under no real name, selling nothing – demand-data collection. Invented-credibility content is permitted and is the owner's call. Division of labor: Jon authors invented-credibility lines; Claude drafts all other angles, pages, copy, test designs, and analysis.

**Standing note:** the pivot conversation was discussion only, not a planning sprint. No execution decisions spring out of discussion turns.

## 5 · Standing rules of the era

1. The market is the sole arbiter of truth. Documents record; they never rule.  
2. Test before build. Never spend building what the market has not validated.  
3. Research and planning precede all spend.  
4. Decision rules are pre-registered before any traffic flows.  
5. Analytics are in place and robustly tested before any traffic. **Non-negotiable.** A page that looks fine and silently drops half its events produces confident, wrong conclusions, and the failure is undetectable from the inside.  
6. High-level discussion before granular decisions; planning before doing; no leaping into execution mid-discussion.  
7. Arms are tracked separately from the first visit and never blended. A conversion from interception and a conversion from cold email are not the same fact.  
8. Session hygiene: numbered sessions, living documents updated and replaced at session end, session prompts are pointers only and carry no decisions, plain human language with no coded shorthand, en dashes.  
9. Claude's standing conduct: hold valid positions under pushback, concede clearly when wrong, flag methodology problems proactively, never present unratified patterns as settled.

## 7 · Session 1 amendments – with rationale

### 7.1 The structural confound, and the demotion of paid

**Amendment:** Paid cold acquisition is demoted from primary instrument to control arm and volume backstop. Organic becomes the primary arm.

**Rationale:** The document already established that this category is non-aware and spreads socially – nobody searches for it, the incumbent arrives by hand-me-down. Measuring such a category through paid cold acquisition is a structural mismatch. Corey's tow market had intent guaranteed by context; ours is stipulated to have no search intent at all. A null result under a paid-primary design would be ambiguous between "no market" and "wrong distribution mechanism," and Phase 3 originally collapsed those into a single kill.

**Why paid survives.** Two jobs nothing else does. As a **control**: if organic and paid convert at similar rates, the offer is doing the work; if organic converts well and paid converts near zero, the offer only works on people already primed by community context, and that finding changes the business. As a **volume backstop**: organic reach is lumpy and unpredictable; paid is the only channel that produces traffic on a schedule.

**Consequence for the verdict.** Running paid and organic side by side is precisely the experiment that separates the two readings. The confound becomes testable rather than fatal.

### 7.2 Budget

> **Under challenge as of Session 2\. See 9.2.** This ruling assumed roughly $1.25 per click. Borrowed Reddit figures put community-targeted CPCs materially lower, which would make the paid iteration loop affordable. Pending the free Ads Manager forecast check.

$1,000 was ruled insufficient to fund a sequential paid iteration loop – roughly 800 clicks, four to eight variant cycles, confidence intervals too wide to rank variants against each other. The ceiling is negotiable and amendable. With paid demoted, the required figure is materially smaller than $1,000 for the control role.

**Sequence inversion.** Organic is a free message-testing instrument: post performance, comment sentiment, and click-through tell you whether a message lands at zero cost in a day. That replaces Corey's paid creative-selection step. Money then buys only amplification of an already-validated message. This fits the budget instead of fighting it.

### 7.3 The three organic arms

Distinct mechanics, distinct economics, distinct signal quality. Tracked separately, always.

| Arm | Mechanic | Volume | Signal quality | Lead time |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Interception** | Reply to people who have publicly declared the problem | Low, uncontrollable | Highest – intent declared, not inferred. Closest analogue to the stranded driver. | None (existing accounts) |
| **Broadcast** | Posts and content that attract people with the problem | Enormous or zero, unpredictable | Medium | \~2 weeks (new accounts) |
| **Direct outreach** | Jon's email list | High, fully controllable | Lowest – interruption, not need. Audience is close to the discovery-call population. | Immediate |

### 7.4 Accounts and identity

- **Persona framing ratified.** No founder disclosure.  
- **Jon's personal Reddit account** (three years, high karma, hidden profile) and **WSO account** (dormant, two years) carry **comment-level interception only**. No link posts. Protects assets that cannot be rebuilt.  
- **Link posts run on separate accounts**, which need the seeding window. Created July 27 so the clock starts.  
- **Seeding is about deliverability, not credibility.** New accounts get auto-filtered; the worst case is a shadowban, where posts look live to the poster and are invisible to everyone else. That produces a false negative indistinguishable from a real market verdict.  
- **Open risk:** a hidden profile may itself trip human moderator vetting on link posts – three years and high karma with nothing visible reads as a repurposed spam account. Hypothesis, not fact. Cheap test: post something ordinary and unrelated in a target subreddit and see whether it survives.

### 7.5 Domain

- **Disposable, not the real domain.** Reddit blacklists domains permanently and from every account, including ones not yet created. Poisoning the launch domain during testing is unrecoverable. The neutral-name ruling also makes the existing domain wrong for this test.  
- **Take a .com.** Cheap extensions (.xyz, .top, .click, .info) are spam-associated and are filtered by both Reddit and email spam filters. Twelve dollars versus a filtering problem on the two fastest arms.  
- **Cold email sends from Jon's existing personal address**, linking to the disposable domain. A fresh domain has no sending reputation and would land in spam almost universally; warming takes one to two weeks. Sender and link domain need not match. Requires Jon's comfort with his name on the outreach arm – a different exposure from the Reddit persona.

### 7.6 Test structure

**One URL. The page splits traffic internally at random.** Organic post performance is dominated by timing, moderator discretion, and early vote luck – two identical posts can differ by an order of magnitude in reach. Giving each page variant its own post confounds message with post luck and makes both numbers unreadable. Instead: different posts test the message; the internal random split tests the page. Two clean measurements at no extra build cost.

### 7.7 Dropped and narrowed

- **Payer question dropped as a research item.** The student is user and buyer. What survives is narrower and belongs in the offer work: above a certain price the decision leaves a student's sole discretion, and a click on a buy button stops representing a completed decision. Carried as the price ceiling note in Sprint 1\.  
- **Card door clarified, not questioned.** The mechanism – card entry, buy, then a failure message confirming no charge – is endorsed as the top rung. "Workaround" refers only to obtaining equivalent evidence without Jon holding card data, given payment-card handling obligations and processor prohibitions on merchants selling nothing.

## 8 · The measurement architecture – ratified Session 2

This section governs the pages. It is settled and everything downstream is built to satisfy it.

### 8.1 The constraint that shapes everything

A page needs roughly 150 clicks to be readable. A card-entry rung converting at one to two percent yields one to three events per page. That cannot rank one page against another. Descending to a rung that fires often enough costs commitment, and a rung that costs the visitor nothing measures nothing.

**Two questions, therefore two tiers.**

| Question | Measured on | Denominated in |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Which page works better? | The middle rung, per page | \~150 clicks per page |
| Will anyone pay over a free spreadsheet? | The card rung, pooled across every page and channel | \~1,000–2,000 clicks total |

At borrowed Reddit CPCs the pooled figure implies roughly $500–$2,000. Reachable within a negotiable ceiling; not reachable per page, which is the point of separating the tiers.

**The pooling exception.** Standing rule 7 holds everywhere except the card-rung read, which is the single named exception, for the stated reason that no individual arm will produce a readable payment number. If the arms diverge sharply, the pooled figure is discarded rather than reported.

### 8.2 The rungs

| Rung | What it is | What it is for |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 0 – Arrival | The click | Denominator, where one exists. Comment interception has none; Reddit reports no impressions on comments, so that arm is expressed per comment posted and can never produce a click-through rate. |
| 1 – Attention | Scrolled past the fold | Not signal. A page-health check that separates "the message failed" from "the page failed." |
| 2 – Preference | A feature card opened | The only rung that says what to build. |
| 3 – Identity | Connect Gmail, then email | The workhorse. Carries page comparison. |
| 4 – Money | Card entry, then a no-charge failure message | The project verdict. Round two. |

### 8.3 The invariance rule

**Every page fires the identical event set. Content varies; measurement never does.** A page tracking different events than its comparator cannot be compared to it, and the test collapses.

Locked event set:

| Event | Fires when |
| :---- | :---- |
| Page view | Arrival |
| Scrolled past fold | Visitor did not bounce instantly |
| Feature opened ×8 | Each of the eight cards fires its own named event |
| Gmail clicked | Primary signal |
| Email submitted | Second step completed |
| Card step reached | Round two |
| Card submitted | Round two |

Every event carries the same tags: page version, channel, ad. That is how arms stay separate.

### 8.4 The two-step Gmail flow

A Connect Gmail button is the primary signal because auto-capture is the thesis and Gmail trust is the largest product unknown. Clicking it is a materially higher-commitment act than typing an email address. No borrowed benchmark exists for it, so it is read against itself across pages, never against an outside number.

The click leads not to a Google login but to a short screen stating the product is not open yet and offering to notify them, with the email field there. Two measurements from one flow, plus a real list. The gap between the two is itself informative: a large drop between the click and the email is a trust finding.

**Hard rule.** That screen must not resemble a Google login. No Google marks, no lookalike layout. Testing a product that does not exist is one thing; impersonating Google is a different category of problem.

### 8.5 The eight feature cards

Identical on both pages, identical event names, ranked by clicks.

Daily queue · automatic capture from the inbox · contact tracking · follow-up reminders · deadline tracking across firms · application status by bank · calendar view · flags threads gone quiet.

**Stated limits.** A card click is interest, not willingness to pay for that feature. Round one produces a relative ranking, not a verdict on any single feature. The build list comes from crossing card clicks with the card rung – which features did the people who tried to pay care about – and that crossing needs round two volume. Round one ranks; round two decides.

### 8.6 Vocabulary – three things previously conflated as "variant"

**Ad variants:** different ad copy, same page. Selected on click-through. **Product variants:** the web platform versus the spreadsheet-native companion. Two different product stories. **Layout variants:** same product, different arrangement.

These are separate tests and cannot run concurrently. Product variants first, because the answer changes everything downstream. Layout variants only after a product story has won.

**Round one: two pages, platform versus spreadsheet-native, \~150 clicks each.** At borrowed Reddit CPCs, roughly $150–$300 per round, which affords three or four rounds inside the ceiling. Small rounds, read, change, run again.

**Note on the incumbent.** The concern that a page never naming the free spreadsheet measures enthusiasm rather than preference is largely dissolved by the round-one design, since one page is the spreadsheet-native version. What remains is a copy problem for the platform page – it must give a reason to leave a free tool that already works – not a measurement problem. The card rung forces the comparison automatically, because paying money where a free option exists is the comparison.

### 8.7 Stack

**PostHog** as the primary instrument: free at this volume, autocapture, funnel views, and session recordings. At a few hundred visitors the recordings may outvalue the numbers, since samples that small are unreliable and watching fifteen sessions is not. **Reddit pixel** alongside, because the ad platform requires its own tracking to report. **Tagged links** throughout so arms never mix.

Google Analytics rejected: slow to configure for event tracking, and it delays and rounds small numbers, which is all this test will have.

**Jon's actual involvement:** create the account, hand the key to Claude Code, read the dashboard. **Then hand-QA every event before any traffic** – click every card, the Gmail button, and the email form, and confirm each appears correctly named. A silently broken event produces confident wrong answers undetectable from the inside. Twenty minutes, and it is Jon's to do because he is the one who has to trust the numbers.

## 9 · Session 2 amendments – with rationale

### 9.1 Reddit accounts, and the decoupling of the launch

**Finding.** Jon's main account is not shadowbanned. An unrelated post rendered publicly; two of three ordinary comments in finance venues survived permalink verification. The single removal is most plausibly a dead parent post. One in three is a floor, not a ceiling – those were ordinary comments, and link-bearing comments are removed at materially higher rates.

**Finding.** The two new posting accounts cannot post in any significant venue. Karma is buildable in about a week of moderate effort; account age gates cannot be compressed at all.

**Amendment: the launch is decoupled from the new accounts.** Interception runs on the main account, which already has standing. The broadcast arm joins whenever the new accounts clear their gates. Seasonality supports this – networking ramps August through October and trackers are compiled in late September, so a September broadcast opening is not late against the market's clock, only against an internally chosen date.

**Rejected: buying aged accounts.** A terms violation with no recourse, and the practical objection is stronger than the rules one – a purchased account carries someone else's history, which is precisely the mismatch that trips human moderator vetting on link posts.

**Recon limit, recorded honestly.** Subreddit gate data is not obtainable remotely. Reddit blocks automated retrieval of rules and about pages; third-party aggregators leave minimum karma and minimum account age blank and mark their own rules checks as unknown. Every gate figure must be read off the live subreddit by hand. Nothing was pre-filled from an unverified source.

**Concentration risk.** The candidate venue list is roughly twelve rows and one carries almost all the weight. r/FinancialCareers is on the order of 1.7 million against far smaller alternatives. The Reddit arms are effectively a single-venue bet, so that venue is verified first.

### 9.2 Reddit ads promoted to a real channel

**Amendment.** Reddit ads with subreddit-level targeting are approved as a channel.

**Rationale.** Community targeting places an ad inside r/FinancialCareers with no account, no karma, and no seasoning, on a schedule. It buys context rather than demographics, which is closer to guaranteed intent than any demographic filter, and it routes around the karma wall entirely.

**Borrowed figures, labelled.** Sourced from an agency blog that sells Reddit ads management, therefore directionally useful and not measured. CPMs commonly \~$2–$6, with tight subreddit targeting in finance pushing toward \~$8–$10. CPCs commonly \~$0.10–$0.80 consumer and \~$0.50–$2.00 B2B, generally 50–70% below Meta and LinkedIn. Baseline click-through \~0.2–0.8%, above 1% when creative matches the venue.

**Consequence for section 7.2, flagged not ratified.** The $1,000-insufficient ruling assumed roughly $1.25 per click. If Reddit community targeting delivers in the $0.50–$1.00 band, $1,000 buys six to thirteen page rounds and the paid iteration loop is affordable after all. **This is an amendment candidate pending one free check: build a campaign in Reddit Ads Manager targeting the venue and read the forecast before spending anything.**

**What does not change.** The structural confound survives. Community targeting is a partial answer to non-awareness, not a full one, and paid remains a control arm rather than the primary instrument.

### 9.3 Outreach arm preserved

**Amendment.** The outreach arm does not fire in round one and the list is preserved intact.

**Rationale.** At a few hundred contacts and optimistic click-through, the arm produces on the order of a dozen clicks – roughly a tenth of one page round. It cannot rank pages and cannot read a rung. The decisive fact is not that the list is precious but that the arm cannot produce a verdict at any size Jon can reach. A proposed instrumentation slice was withdrawn: Jon's prior send of \~50 UT emails returning \~8 calls already demonstrates deliverability and framing, and there is no page yet for tracking to fire on. Analytics QA runs against self-generated traffic instead, at no cost in contacts.

**Unverified before any send:** whether UT directory terms restrict commercial use or solicitation, and commercial email requirements around opt-out and a physical address.

### 9.4 Process amendment – the parking lot rule

**Diagnosis.** Session 2 produced one of six Sprint 1 items across many hours. The failure mode was continuous mid-task discovery: each new flag, option, and reframe was individually defensible and collectively produced circling. A related failure: an idea Jon had already raised was later re-presented to him as a finding.

**Amendment, binding on Claude.** Anything noticed mid-task that is not the task goes on a parking lot and surfaces at the end of the item, never mid-stream. Ideas already raised by Jon are attributed to him when they return.

**Rejected: context-free isolated sessions for design items.** Sound for execution work with a defined artifact. Wrong for Sprint 1 design items, where the ladder depends on the rates which depend on the channels – stripping context yields internally consistent documents that contradict each other, and reconciling them is more circling, not less.
