# Blotter IB – IDEAS Ledger
Scope creep lands here, not in the build. Created July 16, 2026 (session 2).

- **Outreach-pacing nudges** ("you've already emailed someone at Goldman TMT this week"). Parked by owner ruling July 16 s2: the platform NEVER enforces or encodes pacing norms – user discretion is a standing product principle. Revisit only if pacing proves to be an undeniable recruiting norm months out.
- **Bank tiers (BB/EB/MM/Boutique)** as user-set optional tags on bank entities. Cut from September (curated/subjective/display-only).
- **Gamification / community stats** (carried from inventory 1.18).
- **Contact rotation within a bank** (carried, parked 1.17 – note: distinct from pacing nudges; rotation suggests NEW contacts, pacing restricts frequency).
- **Learned email-pattern suggestions** (from OPEN-4 kill): suggest an address variant only when the user's own confirmed-valid addresses at that bank support it; tombstone-aware; zero curation. Revisit much later on interest.
- **Personal stats dashboard**: emails logged, coffee chats held, banks applied to – motivation view. Cheap, no cold-start. Not core (owner, s2).
- **Community stats / leaderboard**: per-category averages across users, opt-in. Cold-start + privacy questions. Not core (owner, s2).
- **"Banks I'm actively networking with" view** (residue of the killed networking indicator – only plausible surviving use).
- **LLM inbound classification** (declined at inventory 3.2, s3): an inference layer classifying inbound mail as recruiting/not, as an upgrade over the deterministic bank-domain watch. Declined because it's real build surface, per-email inference cost, a harder privacy conversation, and an uninspectable false-negative rate – while buying ~nothing over the domain watch for the actual scenario. Revisit only if capture is live and the domain watch demonstrably misses real mail.
- **BCC send-logging** (killed at inventory 4.12, s4): zero-OAuth outbound confirmation via a per-user BCC address. Dominated in every current branch by capture (live) or the manual buttons (contingency); adds a user behavior to a product that removes them. Historical record only.
- **Template expansion beyond initial outreach + bulk merge** (declined at inventory 4.9, s4): merge for follow-ups/thank-yous and any batch-generation of outreach. Declined on positioning ("not mass outreach") and personalization etiquette; one-at-a-time structurally enforces the hook line. Revisit on explicit user signal only.

- **Layer 1 templates \+ mechanical merge \+ the emergence model** (killed at inventory 4.9 s6, CONFIRMED s7 – formal tombstone): the reusable template library, merge-field substitution ({FirstName}, {Bank}), the day-one starter set, and templates emerging from the user's own repeated writing – all dead together. Grounds: the 2.8 precedent transferred (real job, commodity territory – Gmail templates/Mixmax/YAMM own the slot; real build surface; positioning blur against "pure logistics, not an outreach tool"); trigger surface was marginal anyway (initial outreach generates no queue item per 1.16 – merge would have fired off the main stage). The wall it bought: "Blotter never writes your outreach – not with AI, not with templates. It gets you to the send window; the words are yours." **Resurrection condition: strong, explicit user signal only** – users demonstrably keeping template stashes elsewhere and asking for them in-product. Deep links (Layer 0) survive as pure convenience.

- **Competitor vocabulary reference – Superday AI (parked s8):** their manual-CRM enumerations, kept as *vocabulary inspiration only* – Status: New / Reached Out / Phone Call / Coffee Chat / Following Up / Connected (note: muddles interaction types with pipeline stages – corroborates Blotter's computed one-axis model, do NOT import as states); Source: Cold Outreach / Alumni / Club Event / Referral; Seniority: Analyst / Associate / VP / Director / MD; Group list: TMT / Healthcare / FIG / Industrials / Consumer-Retail / Natural Resources / Real Estate / M&A / Restructuring / Sponsors / Capital Markets / Energy / Infrastructure / Business Services. Possible future inputs to interaction-type log vocabulary, origin/tag suggestions, or group/division pickers – all inventory territory, none ruled, zero debts. Competitive note: Superday is learning-first with a dumb CRM attached – the positioning mirror image; validates the no-learning wall.

*Note: the "logistics layer" marketing/positioning thesis is NOT an idea – it's a live D6/D7 input, recorded in roadmap.md (D6) and feature-inventory.md (standing principles).*

- **Sheets-Native Companion ("Blotter in your spreadsheet")** (registered s4-close; PRODUCT parked – the s4-registered D11 experiment tests demand before any build). A stripped-down modality living inside the user's own Google Sheet: Sheets API + OAuth (not a Chrome extension); the import ingest layer (LLM column mapping) reads their messy tracker and generates a SECOND tab in the same spreadsheet – clean, Blotter-formatted, color-coded, LIVE via Gmail capture (last-email dates, reply status, staleness coloring, follow-up fields auto-populate). Lacks everything else: no platform UI, no queue, no calendar, no to-dos. ~Half platform price. Aimed at the Group 2 identity-performance niche (prestigious rigid-process orgs where the spreadsheet is banker-archetype costume; migration may be rejected even when a superior tool is shown). Build reuse: import + capture as-is; new work = tab generation/formatting + freshness. Key architectural dodge: output writes to OUR tab, never edits theirs in place – avoids the hard two-way-sync class. CASA note: Sheets scope is sensitive-not-restricted (Gmail capture still carries the intermediary strategy's obligations). Open by design: freshness model (re-ingest cadence vs. writable tab); unit economics at half price with full capture COGS; cannibalization vs. Trojan-horse funnel; and the core identity premise – untested in both directions, the discovery dataset cannot adjudicate. The D11 experiment exists to test the premise BEFORE any of these questions matter.
