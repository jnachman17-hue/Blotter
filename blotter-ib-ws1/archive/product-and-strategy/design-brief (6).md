## §2 – STAGE 0: THE REFERENCE-COLLECTION FRAMEWORK (RATIFIED s7)

**Governing principle: collect by design problem, never by product.** A screenshot is useful only if it answers a specific question Blotter must also answer. The annotations are the real output – extracted patterns, not an image pile.

**Storage:** one folder per problem, files named `product_problem_note.png` (e.g. `linear_grouped-list_status-dots.png`). **Every screenshot gets one annotation line: what specifically is good here and what we take.** A screenshot with no note is dead weight in three weeks.

**AMENDED s8 (owner ruling): the folder framework below is RETIRED.** Two rulings: (1) division of labor – Claude pulls references inline and does all annotation/extraction; the owner's job is the taste verdict only ("that one / too busy / warmer"), because analysis is a trained skill and the verdict is the founder's; (2) Stage 0 closed-compressed – the s8 harvest below is sufficient to author tokens; remaining reference needs (quiet steppers, quick-add, filter menus, soft-red calibration, strip-agenda calendars, empty states) are pulled **just-in-time** when their surface/token is being designed, not front-loaded. The 13-folder list is retained below as the map of *what* still needs referencing, not *how*.

**§2a – HARVESTED PATTERN STATEMENTS (s8 – cite these in generation prompts):**

1. **Weight-based anchoring (Linear, verified live):** the anchor fact reads near-black medium-weight while every sibling demotes to gray – the anchor wins because neighbors are quiet, never by size inflation. → Contact name / bank-name anchors this way.
2. **Single-location classification (Linear, verified live):** the state word lives in exactly one place – the group header carries the word, rows repeat only a glyph, never both. → Validates the existing §3 pair: grouped queue = sections classify, no per-item dot (1.1); flat contacts list = dot+word per row (2.4). The word goes wherever the grouping isn't.
3. **Quiet contained table (Superday AI \+ owner prototype, owner-liked twice):** one contained card (surface step, soft border, radius), **no vertical rules**, faint hairlines \+ generous padding for separation, quiet hover. Columns may exist as alignment; the spreadsheet *tell* is visible cell/grid structure, and that is what's rejected. → Owner's standing reaction pattern: rejected Attio, Folk, and dense Linear for visible grid/furniture; accepted the same information rendered quietly.

## §3 – STAGE 2 RENDERING RULINGS (ALL RULED s7 – the surface ledgers)

Every item below is an owner ruling from the session-7 sitting, decided macro → micro through the five-slot framework (job / must-hold / open questions / constraints / reference needs). The must-hold content and constraints per surface are extractions from feature-inventory.md and are not restated exhaustively here – the inventory remains the mechanics authority.

### Surface 2 – CRM (planned first: it is the data spine; Surfaces 1 and 3 are computed views over its objects)

**Job:** where the user sees and manages the full truth of their recruiting network and pipeline – every person, bank, application, with status they never maintain by hand. The spreadsheet made you maintain the picture; Surface 2 presents it.

1. **Page architecture:** ONE top-level CRM destination with internal tabs **Contacts | Applications** (nav stays three items: Queue / CRM / Calendar; user-facing nav label is a copy decision → design spec). **Applications organize BY BANK** – grouped under bank headers, never a flat list; bank headers are click-targets to the bank page; multi-city duplicates (2.13) sit as visible siblings under their bank.
2. **List ↔ record pattern (closes inventory 2.16):** **contacts open in a SIDE PANEL** (list stays visible; high-frequency low-dwell lookup workflow); **banks/applications open as FULL PAGES** (heavy structural content; low-frequency high-dwell work). Re-derived from content weight + workflow, not inherited from phase-1. **NO expand-to-full-page affordance** – the panel is the contact record's entire home (owner: keep it simple); consequence: the panel's internal hierarchy carries full weight.
3. **The contacts list:** **list rows** (composed hierarchy, not table columns – differentiated from the spreadsheet while keeping density; a skeletal referral stub reads fine as a sparse row). Row = **name (anchor, strongest weight) · bank · title (quieter) · state dot+word · last interaction** ("last emailed Jan 12" – the engine's evidence ambient in the list). NO badges in-row. **Flat by default**, alphabetical; grouping one keystroke away via sort. Controls: **search · sort (alphabetical / bank / state / last interaction) · one filter button · Add contact.** **Filter set (ruled): Bank · State · Tags · Needs valid email · Outstanding intros.** Met-in-person: demoted to panel-only fact (decorative as a filter – owner concurrence); referred-filter cut (redundant with outstanding-intros, which is the job that matters – a dropped intro is a burned favor). Referral statuses (outstanding/dismissed/completed) remain real, visible on the record; only outstanding is promoted to a filter. Rendering copy is always plain language – "Needs valid email," never kebab-case.
4. **State rendering (feeds Stage 1 directly):** **colored dot + word** (Linear pattern – color scans, word teaches, nothing shouts). Filled chips are RESERVED for tags so computed truth and user vocabulary never look like the same species. **Semantic color mapping: Not yet contacted = gray · Awaiting their reply = blue · You owe a reply = amber · Concluded = green · No response = SOFT RED.** Owner ruling on the red: emotional color is permitted – no-guilt is a COPY rule, not a color rule; red-as-information ("this thread is dead," the owner's own spreadsheet convention) is legitimate; the calibration knob is softness – muted/desaturated, "ended, honestly," tuned by eye in design-reference.html. Consequence: red is not exclusively reserved for alarm → the two-red question goes to Stage 1 (§4).
5. **Contact panel stack (three zones):** **Header** (always visible): name, title, bank-as-link, location; state dot+word + the evidence line ("Last emailed Jan 12 – silent 6 days"); email addresses with validity + LinkedIn (the action facts). **Working body: NOTES FIRST, then interaction timeline** (owner ruling – lived workflow: pre-call prep reads notes; the computed timeline is context, not centerpiece). Small **"+ log interaction"** at the timeline's head (1.13's override principle; the panel's one action affordance). **Quiet zone** (bottom): tags (chips, addable), referral structure both directions, degree/school + group/division, the two dial overrides LAST (settings, not workspace – labeled per 1.3's findability, but low).
6. **Unified bank page** (owner ruling – Option A; the application has no separate page): **header** = bank name + rolled-up situation line ("2 applications · 1 Interviewing, 1 Submitted · 4 contacts"). **Main column** = applications as blocks: stage machine as a **quiet horizontal stepper** (small nodes, current filled, future ghosted; **terminals are ACTIONS/buttons, never nodes** – "rejection is never a pipeline node," 2.11); deadline + escalation; dated obligations; documents; portal username; cities + duplicate-for-city; archived applications collapse to one expandable quiet row. **Side column** = the bank's contacts as compact rows (same row design, narrower); click → panel slides over the page. **Quiet bottom** = bank facts: normalized name, learned domains (read-only – capture machinery, not user furniture), free notes. Rationale recorded: most banks carry one application; city-siblings want to be seen together; one heavyweight page design instead of two.
7. **Add-contact flow:** **quick-add popover** – fields **name · bank (normalized picker + free-text escape) · email (optional; absence routes to needs-valid-email by design) · title** – save-and-add-another for batch entry + one **"more details"** link that saves and opens the panel. Dedup soft-warning fires here, non-blocking. **referred_by deliberately excluded** – referral stubs are born from the post-call flow (2.6), not cold adds; the escape hatch covers edge cases. Full form page ruled out (punishes the fresh-start batch session the beachhead is defined by).

### Surface 1 – Daily Queue

**Job:** answer "what do I owe right now?" in one glance, every morning, zero maintenance.

1. **Queue item = two-line row.** Line 1: **the human's name** (strongest weight) + item-type marker + **quiet snooze + three-dots**, right-aligned. Line 2: **the full WHY sentence, never truncated**, calm secondary type – the engine's soul gets the space the philosophy demands; density solves a problem this page (3–8 items) doesn't have. Cards ruled out (task-app energy, gamification-adjacent). **NO state dot on items** – sections classify, items act; one classification voice per page.
2. **CAPTURE-FIRST RENDERING (s7 in-session correction, recorded permanently):** the item's primary affordance is the **deep link – tap the row, Gmail opens** (send-class items; body always empty per 4.9 Layer 0). **Clearing is capture's job** – the send is detected, the item removes itself (the moment the motion mock dramatizes). **Manual clear lives in the three-dots** (mark sent / mark replied / log missed call), quiet, permanent per 1.13. **The design spec describes the capture-state UI as THE design; prominent "I sent it ✓" confirm buttons are documented as the temporary manual-contingency rendering ONLY** – never the baseline. This correction exists because the session initially designed the contingency as the product; it must not recur.
3. **Context strip:** horizontal band of small event chips above the sections ("Thu · Goldman superday · 2pm"), capped per 3.6, tap-through to Surface 3; **renders nothing when empty.** Structurally different in kind from the vertical debt list – passive chips, not a seventh section.
4. **Sections = CONTAINED GROUPS** (owner ruling from rendered variants, judged by eye in-session): each section a quietly bounded card – background step + hairline border + radius – with the label INSIDE kept small and muted; items separated by hairlines within. Chosen over quiet-labels-plus-hairlines (blur risk on full days) and over loud header bars (furniture). **The grammar propagates: the Applications tab's bank groups render with the same containment pattern** – one grouping language product-wide. **NO counts in headers** (the eye counts 3 items faster than reading "· 3"; a count is a debt scoreboard). **Schedule-section variant:** time leads ("2:00 pm – Call with Sarah Chen · Goldman"), no snooze, tap opens event/contact – same component, different leading fact.
5. **Sunday pile:** persistent quiet footer link **with count** ("Sunday pile · 4 waiting" – the one legitimate count: collapsed content is exactly what counts are for); hidden when empty; gains one degree of presence on Sundays per 1.14.
6. **Page shape:** **centered single column, max-width capped** (`--width-queue`, Stage 1 token, ~640–760px class) – the queue is a morning read, not a workspace. Surface 2 stays wide (lists + two-column bank page earn the canvas). Empty state: "Clear – nothing owed today," no confetti (1.15).

### Surface 3 – Calendar

**Job:** the week's recruiting shape – events and bank-imposed dates merged in the one view Google structurally cannot produce. Planning surface, not action surface. View-only on Google throughout.

1. **View = STRIP + AGENDA HYBRID** (owner ruling – Option D): a compact day strip on top (spatial anchor; days mark events and deadlines with distinct dots; click-to-scroll) over a day-grouped agenda list (the detail carrier). Month grid ruled out (80% empty boxes at recruiting density reads "nothing happening" – false and demoralizing); hour-grid week ruled out (scheduling machinery with the scheduling amputated – view-only). The strip is Surface 1's context strip at full width – same information grammar, one product language. **Strip window: TWO WEEKS – penciled by Claude (superday-season 10-day horizons), owner-overridable to seven on a word.**
2. **Deadlines = DIFFERENTIATED ROWS** (owner ruling – Option A): events lead with time; deadlines lead with a glyph + "Due," no time slot. Same row component, two dressings. Day-header attachment ruled out (breaks under compile-window stacking – the worst case decides). **Final-week escalation = the row warming through the ALARM-red family** – Surface 3 is alarm-red's legitimate home (consistent with the two-red note, §4).
3. **Quiet affordances:** manual-add = small "+" in the page header (3.5). Claim path = collapsed line after the agenda's end ("3 recent events not shown · review"), renders only when unmatched events exist, one-tap claim, teaches mappings silently (3.1). Never a banner, never a nav badge.

- **Architecture:** recruiting-only, view-only – matched events merged with application deadlines, nothing else; compact day strip over a day-grouped agenda; no month grid, no hour grid.

- **Strip dots (21a):** dots mark **species presence, never count** – a five-item day shows at most one event mark + one deadline mark. **All dots solid; no legend, ever** ("if a mark needs a key, the mark is wrong"). Deadline = solid ink · event = solid muted gray · deadline ≤2 days = solid alarm-red `#B4483B`. Escalation is a color channel only – the countdown stays caption voice and changes hue.
- **Strip is a jump control, not a filter** – the agenda continues past the 14-day window (a beyond-window February deadline still lists).
- **Within-day ordering law:** deadlines lead the day, then timed events chronologically; same-day deadlines alphabetical by bank (1.0's tie-break, applied to the surface).
- **Title = the date range (19f)** – the word "Calendar" dropped; the nav already says it. Deadline rows lead with Due, no time slot.
