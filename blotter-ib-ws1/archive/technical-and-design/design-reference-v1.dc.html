Character statement (ratified s9 — heads every generation prompt)

Blotter IB looks like a clean instrument, not a dashboard costume: a navy-anchored, cool-neutral surface where a person's name is always the heaviest thing on screen and every computed fact speaks in a quiet caption voice – small, even-numbered, unmistakably the machine's. One contained card, hairlines not grids, air not furniture. Nothing shouts, nothing gamifies, nothing decorates – color appears only where it carries state, and red is information, never blame. Calm, credible, finance-literate without cosplay.
