Banner safe areas. On both X and LinkedIn your profile photo overlaps the lower-left of the banner on desktop, and the sides get cropped on mobile. Every banner here keeps its content centred and well inside those zones, so nothing important gets covered or cut. Company pages are different. A company cover renders at roughly 5.86:1, so a 1584 × 396 banner uploaded there loses a band off the top and the bottom, and the page’s logo tile covers the lower left — a bigger bite than a profile photo, starting higher up, landing exactly where the mark sits. Use the 1128 × 191 headline-only banners on a company page.
One thing to rule on. The gradient banners come in two versions. The cool one runs the manual-zone blue twice, corner to corner. The warm one is the landing page verbatim, which means it carries the maintained-zone cream #fbeac6. lib/brand.ts ratifies cream as semantic — it means “Blotter maintains this” — and keeps it out of identity assets, and a banner is an identity asset. The warm version is here to be looked at, not because it is approved.
